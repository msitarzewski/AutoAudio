At home on your Mac

A studio that
stays close.

The interesting part is the question. The software should look after the work around it.

Room for your own setup

Studio Setup finds supported local engines and walks through writing, narration and a short listening sample. Connected research and writing are optional; their provider limits remain separate.

Your work, saved along the way

Questions, research, scripts and accepted narration are saved as the book takes shape. Completed stages can be reused when a run needs to resume.

A book with its trail attached

Source notes and automatic listening reports stay with an edition. The checks keep their findings visible, including passages that need a closer listen.

Where the project is today +

The Mac studio has produced a complete book through its local pipeline, including native Library playback. AutoAudio is still a development project.

Companion listening on iPhone, iPad and Apple Vision Pro, and optional private iCloud sync, are in development and qualification. Public app downloads are not posted here yet.
